Expression Grammar

+-expression-------------------------------------+
|   o-- term ( ( "+" | "-" ) term )* --o         |
+------------------------------------------------+

+-term-------------------------------------------+
|   o-- factor ( ( "*" | "/" ) factor )* --o     |
+------------------------------------------------+

+-factor-----------------------------------------+
|   o-- number | "(" expression ")" --o          |
+------------------------------------------------+

+-number-----------------------------------------+
|   o-- digit+ --o                               |
+------------------------------------------------+

+-digit------------------------------------------+
|   o-- "0" | "1" | "2" | "3" --o                |
+------------------------------------------------+
