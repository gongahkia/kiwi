# Confidence Tiers

wat confidence tiers describe source strength, not truth.

## T1

Rule: two or more canonical sources support the same expansion and meaning.

## T2

Rule: one canonical source supports the expansion and meaning.

## T3

Rule: one or more non-canonical sources support the expansion and meaning.

## T4

Rule: user-contributed or unsourced entries. These require moderation before becoming team or public knowledge.
